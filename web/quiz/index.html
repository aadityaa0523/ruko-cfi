The four signs of a scam

  1. Pretends to be someone you trust: bank, police, electricity office, a relative's friend.
  2. Scares or rushes you: "tonight", "in 2 hours", "arrest", "blocked".
  3. Cuts you off: "don't tell anyone", "don't hang up".
  4. Asks for an OTP, PIN, a code, a fee, an app install, or money "sent back".

Real banks and offices never ask for an OTP or PIN, and never threaten you on the phone. When in doubt, hang up and call the official number yourself.
