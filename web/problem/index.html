What it does to people, beyond the money

These are individual cases and one small study. They show what the harm looks like, not how often it happens.

What happenedSource
An elderly couple in Belagavi, Karnataka, died by suicide in March 2025 after losing about ₹50 lakh to a "digital arrest" call (reports give ₹50 to ₹60 lakh). Police traced the accused to Gujarat.Scroll, Deccan Herald
An 82-year-old retired government officer in Pune collapsed and died in October 2025 on finding that he and his wife had lost ₹1.19 crore, their savings, in a "digital arrest" scam.The Bridge Chronicle
A 76-year-old retired doctor in Hyderabad died of a heart attack in September 2025 after being held under "digital arrest" for about three days and made to transfer ₹6.6 lakh.MedBound Times
An IIT Madras-led study of 33 financial-cybercrime victims (12 of them senior citizens) found a state its participants called "mind arrest", which impaired clear thinking during the fraud, followed by shame, insomnia, depression and loss of trust within families. Losses ranged from a few thousand rupees to over ₹1 crore, and victims included doctors, bankers and IT professionals.Careers360, ThePrint
Only 24% of the 2023 LocalCircles survey's fraud-hit families got their money back; 70% of complainants got no resolution.DT Next

If a scam has left you or someone you know in distress, Tele-MANAS (the government's free mental-health helpline) is 14416. To report a fraud, call 1930 within the first hour.

Why Ruko is built the way it is

Most of this fraud is a script spoken to the victim during a call or in a message: pretend to be an authority, scare, cut the person off from family, then ask for an OTP or money. The money leaves during the call. Ruko listens for the script while it is happening, warns in the person's own language, and brings the family in.

What is still unknown

  • How many distinct people are hit each year. Official data counts incidents, and surveys use different samples.
  • How much of the fraud happens by call, versus message, app or website. Ruko's numbers cover all of it; the live-call guard addresses the call part.
  • Whether Ruko changes what people do. That is what the impact page measures, from real use.
